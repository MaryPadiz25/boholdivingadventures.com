Discover Bohol Island

Bohol Island: A Tropical Paradise in the Philippines

Bohol Island is a gem in the heart of the Philippines, offering a unique blend of natural beauty, rich history, and vibrant culture. The iconic Chocolate Hills — over 1,200 conical mounds that turn brown in the dry season — are a UNESCO candidate. Its waters around Panglao Island host some of the finest dive sites in Southeast Asia: vibrant coral reefs, sea turtles, and whale sharks await beneath the surface.

Beyond diving, explore the Hinagdanan Cave underground lake, hike to Mag-Aso Falls, visit the 16th-century Baclayon Church, or relax on Panglao’s white-sand beaches. Best time to visit for diving: November to April.

How to Get to Bohol Island

✈️ By Air

Direct flights from Manila, Cebu & other cities to Bohol-Panglao International Airport.

⛴️ By Ferry

OceanJet & Lite Ferries from Cebu City Pier 1 to Tagbilaran Port. Around 2 hours journey.

🚐 Private Transfer

Book a private van from Mactan-Cebu Airport to the ferry terminal, then cross to Tagbilaran.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

01What makes Bohol a unique diving destination?

Bohol is home to Balicasag Island — a marine sanctuary with dramatic drop-offs, crystal-clear visibility, massive jackfish schools, and sea turtles. The range of sites suits every skill level.

02When is the best time to dive in Bohol?

Best conditions are from November to May — calm seas, excellent visibility. Diving is possible year-round, though occasional rain showers occur during the wet season.

03What certification do I need?

Most sites require at least an Open Water Diver certification. Advanced OW recommended for deeper dives at Balicasag. Beginners can start with Discover Scuba Diving.

04What dive sites should I not miss?

Balicasag for coral walls & turtles • Pamilacan for reef sharks & mantas • Cabilao for macro life • Anda for untouched reefs • Arco Point for cave diving.

05How safe is diving in Bohol?

Bohol diving is very safe with experienced guides and proper protocols. Emergency decompression chambers are available in Cebu City. Always dive within your certification limits.

06Can I combine diving with other activities?

Absolutely! Bohol offers the Chocolate Hills, Tarsier sanctuaries, island hopping, snorkeling, waterfalls — perfect for non-divers in your group too.

07Are PADI courses available?

Yes — from Discover Scuba through Open Water, Advanced, Rescue, Enriched Air and up to Divemaster. All courses include full equipment.

08What is included in the packages?

All packages include full scuba equipment. Own equipment gives a discount (PHP 300–6,000 off). Environmental fees are separate and support marine conservation.
